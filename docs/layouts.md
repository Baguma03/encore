# Layouts and appearance
Three layouts are available. `l` cycles through them at runtime (Encore Classic → Classic → Vibrant), `h` cycles back the other way, and `ENCORE_LAYOUT` (or `layout` in the [configuration file](configuration.md)) chooses the starting one.

- **Encore Classic** (default): modelled on the Spotify desktop client's fullscreen mode, with the artist's background image (see [Artist backgrounds](artist-backgrounds.md)). The album art, with rounded corners and a soft shadow, sits on the left with the large title and artist beside it. A rounded-square play/pause button, styled after the Quickshell (illogical-impulse) media widget, sits at the far right with its bottom level with the art's. Along the bottom of the screen runs a thin seekable wavy progress bar with previous and next buttons at its ends, the previous button level with the art's left edge and the next button with the play button's right edge; `s` hides the buttons, and the bar then runs from the art's left edge to the play button's right edge. The elapsed part of the bar is a sine wave that moves while playing and flattens when paused, followed by a vertical bar handle and the flat remaining track; click or drag it to seek. The icons are the filled, rounded Material Symbols, drawn as vector shapes. Clicking anywhere else toggles play/pause. Text, icons, the bar and the button take their colours from the artist image while it is the background, else from the cover (see [Album colours](#album-colours)). The lyrics sit above the title in bold at the same size as in the Vibrant layout, without a shadow; lines differ only in brightness. The top of the active line stays at a fixed height, 60% of the art's height above the art, and a wrapped active line grows downwards. The lines run in a column as wide as the Vibrant layout's lyrics column and wrap where they do there, each line at the column's width scaled by its size, so a lyric breaks at the same words in both layouts; nothing comes closer to the title than one and a half times the gap between the art and the title. Text reaching that edge, or the Up next card's, dissolves into it: the glyphs fade out gradually over about one line's height instead of being cut off. `j` cycles the column's alignment: right-aligned with its right edge on the play button's right edge (the default), left-aligned with its left edge on the title's left edge, centred between the two, or the right-hand column with its lines left-aligned. `o` cycles through three flows for time-synced lyrics:
  - **Stacked** (default), after Apple Music: the active line sits at the top of the stack, brightest, with the next three lines below it, each fainter than the one above. On each new line the stack slides up, the outgoing line fading out as it leaves and the next one brightening into the top slot, with a slight vertical smear while the lines move.
  - **Classic**: four lines: the previous one faint above the active one, the next one faint below it, and the one after that barely visible beneath. On each new line they scroll up smoothly, the outgoing line fading out at the top, the barely visible line brightening into the next line's place and a new one fading in at the bottom.
  - **Scrolling**, like the Classic and Vibrant layouts' lyrics: the whole lyrics scroll past, the active line at its fixed height, larger and brightest, with the lines before and after it smaller and dimmer the further they are from it. On each new line the list scrolls up and the lines grow into or out of the active style.

  `• • •` stands for the time before the first line and for instrumental gaps. Unsynced lyrics use the classic flow and advance through the song at an even pace. While the Up next card (see below) shows, lines above the active one that reach under it dissolve; the active line stays where it is. The `t` date and clock, in the same format as the other layouts, sit centred above the album art.
- **Classic**: modelled on Apple Music's fullscreen player. A blurred album art backdrop, rounded album art with the title, "Artist — Album" and a progress bar below it, clickable previous/play-pause/next buttons, a seekable progress bar, and a time-synced lyrics column on the right. The active lyric line is highlighted, the column scrolls smoothly, and lines far from the active one fade out. An optional date/clock overlay can be toggled with `t`.
- **Vibrant**: modelled on Apple Music's colourful lyrics view. The backdrop is bright: enlarged, heavily blurred copies of the album art with a faint gradient of its main colours over them, cross-fading between songs. Light covers stay light; a soft dark halo behind the text, and a mild wash on very light art, keep white text readable. Square album art sits on the left with the elapsed and remaining time, a thin seekable progress bar, and the title and "Artist — Album" centred beneath it, and the time-synced lyrics are on the right. There are no transport buttons; clicking anywhere toggles play/pause. Tall and square windows stack the art above the lyrics. The `t` clock overlay works here too.
- **Up next**: in every layout, for the last 10% of a song with another one queued, the next song slides into the top-right corner: its rounded cover with its title and artist beside it, and a thin bar below counting down the rest of the current song, drawn straight over the backdrop in the layout's own text colours (with the Vibrant layout's dark halo behind the text). In the Classic and Vibrant layouts, lyric lines reaching under the card fade out while the active line stays put, and tall and square windows move their content down below the card while it shows.
- **Moving backdrop**: enlarged, blurred copies of the album art drift across the screen, turn, swell and blend into each other, like Apple Music's animated artwork. It keeps moving while playback is paused. Toggle it with `b`, or start without it with `ENCORE_MOTION=off`; with it off, the backdrop is still.
- **Backgrounds**: `a` toggles the artist background for the current layout. When it is on and the [artists folder](artist-backgrounds.md) has an image for the song's artist, the layout uses that image: tinted dark in the Encore Classic and Classic layouts with a vignette in the Classic one, and lighter in the Vibrant layout, with a halo behind the text. With motion on, the image slowly pans and zooms. When it is off, or there is no image, each layout uses its own backdrop: blurred album art for Encore Classic (moving when motion is on, darkened to match), dark blurred art for Classic and the colourful art for Vibrant. The setting is kept per layout for the session; it starts on for Encore Classic and off for Classic and Vibrant, and `ENCORE_ARTIST_BG` sets the starting value for all of them (see [Configuration](configuration.md)).

## Album colours
Every layout takes its colours from the image behind it: the album cover, or the artist image whenever it is the background, that is when the [artist background](artist-backgrounds.md) is on for the current layout and the song's artist has an image (from the artists folder, the download cache or a download that finishes while the song plays). Over the blurred or moving album art the colours come from the cover. Both sources go through the same steps below with the same per-layout rules and contrast limits; a black-and-white artist photo gives grey and white colours. The source image's average colour is blended into a small Material 3 dark scheme (primary, on-primary, secondary container and text colours), the same way the Quickshell media widget adapts its colours. The main text is a light pastel of the image's hue, still the brightest text on screen; grey images give near-white text.

- **Encore Classic**: the title, active lyric line, clock and the next song's title use the pastel, the date uses the scheme's muted text colour, and the other lyric lines are fainter versions of it; the artist uses the scheme's muted text colour; the progress bar uses the primary colour for the elapsed part and handle and the secondary container colour for the rest of the track; and the play/pause button is filled with the primary colour while playing and the secondary container colour while paused.
- **Classic**: the title, active lyric line, filled part of the progress bar and all buttons use the pastel; "Artist — Album", the other lyric lines and the times use a slightly deeper shade of it, at their usual opacity. The Up next card takes the title's, subtitle's and progress bar's colours.
- **Vibrant**: as in the Classic layout, but the pastel is mixed towards white until the text keeps at least 80% of white text's contrast against the bright backdrop (70% for the fainter lines).
- **Panels**: the [keyboard shortcuts overlay](../README.md#keyboard-shortcuts), the [clip export panel](export.md#the-panel) and the [lyrics manager](lyrics.md#lyrics-manager) share one palette. The card is a dark surface with a faint hint of the source image's hue, and the dimmed backdrop behind it takes the same hint; titles use the pastel, and descriptions, key caps, outlines and hints are quieter shades of it. The overlay's section headings, selected chips, switches that are on, the Export button, the range slider's fill and handles, and the highlighted lyric lines use the primary colour, with dark text on filled controls. Tooltips and scroll bars inside the export panel follow the same palette.

Every colour keeps a minimum contrast against its backdrop, so light, dark and very saturated images stay readable. The colours cross-fade along with the art when the song changes, and in the same way when their source changes: toggling the artist background with `a`, switching with `l` or `h` to a layout with the other setting, or the artist image arriving after the song started. `k` turns album colours off: the Classic and Vibrant layouts are then plain white, the Encore Classic layout uses a neutral grey scheme with white text, and the panels are neutral grey with white text and white buttons. `ENCORE_TINT=off` starts with them off.
